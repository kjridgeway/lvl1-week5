Protective Spells and Charms

Protego

Protego is the shielding charm. It conjures a magical barrier that will block physical forces and it will deflect or weaken incoming spells. It is a Latin word that means "I protect" or "I defend."

Expecto Patronum

Expecto Patronum is the incantation for the Patronus Charm. It will conjure a magical, silvery-white guardian made of positive memories/energy and protects the caster from dark creatures like Dementors and Lethifolds. It also comes from Latin and means "I await a guardian."

Other protective spells are:

  • Fidelius Charm

    This charm conceals a secret- usually a location- inside a single living person's soul. This makes the place completely invisible and untraceable unless the Secret Kepper reveals it. This kept Harry Potter's parents safe until their secret keeper revealed their location to He-Who-Must-Not-Be-Named.

  • Repello Inimicum

    This spell repels enemies from crossing a magical barrier. This particular spell was combined with Protego Maxima and Fianto Duri to shield Hogwarts during the final battle.

  • Fiano Duri

    This strengthens and hardens other protective sheilds so they can withstand heavy magical assaults.
