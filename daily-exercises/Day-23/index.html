Sidequest: The Forgotten Spellbook

A Mystery Awaits...

Deep within the ancient halls of Hogwarts, a strange disturbance has been reported in the restricted section of the library. An enchanted spellbook has mysteriously disappeared, leaving behind nothing but glowing footprints and a trail of shimmering emerald dust.

The librarian suspects a mischevious magical creature is responsible. Your mission is to investigate the library, uncover the creature's hiding place, and recover the missing spellbook before midnight.

Are you brave enough to accept this quest?
